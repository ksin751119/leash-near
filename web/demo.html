Anyone can call spend. A caller with no rule is refused and kept in its own small ring, so a flood from a stranger can only push out other strangers, never the agents' evidence. A refusal here is part of the story.
Every refusal is a successful transaction. The contract returns the refusal instead of reverting, so the explorer shows a green tick next to a payment that did not happen. That is the design: a revert would roll the record back and leave no reason on-chain. The sentences on this page come from the contract, not from this page.
