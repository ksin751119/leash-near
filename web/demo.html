Not these agents: callers with no rule at all. spend is open to anyone, so the chain refuses them (code 1) and keeps the evidence here.
no stranger has called spend
Every refusal is a successful transaction. The contract returns the refusal instead of reverting, so the explorer shows a transaction with no failed receipt, and a leash: refused log, beside a payment that did not happen. A revert would roll the record back and leave no reason on-chain. Reasons are the contract's own words. Anyone can call spend; strangers get their own small ring so they cannot push out the agents' evidence.
